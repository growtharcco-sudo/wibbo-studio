Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Wibbo Studio is a private tool that uploads videos made by the Wibbo channel to the channel's own YouTube channel and Instagram account. It uses YouTube API Services.

What we access. Only our own YouTube channel and Instagram account, to upload our own videos, set their thumbnails, and delete our own test uploads. We do not access, collect, store or share data about viewers or any other users.

Children. Wibbo videos are made for children and are marked "made for kids" on YouTube. This tool does not collect any personal information from children or anyone else.

Storage. Access keys are kept only in encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and are used only to publish our own videos. You can revoke Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google. By watching our videos on YouTube you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact. Message us on Instagram @hellowibbo.
